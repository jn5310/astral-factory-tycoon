### Long description
You are the last commander on Astra IV, and the only way home is a rocket built from 1,000 parts.

Mine six kinds of ore, lay conveyor lines with a single swipe, and grow a production chain from iron plates to processors and rocket fuel. Keep the power grid balanced, because an overloaded grid slows every machine on it.

When night falls, the locals come for you. Eight alien species attack in growing waves, from fast rushers and acid spitters to wall-breaking bombers and the colossal Leviathan. Feed your turrets with ammo by belt, power your lasers, and watch your best turrets rise from Rookie to Ace. The dirtier your factory runs, the angrier the swarm gets.

A 24-mission campaign teaches you as you play and unlocks four commander powers: call down an Orbital Strike, flood the base with Repair Nanites, Overclock the whole factory, or freeze every alien with a Cryo Pulse. Meteor showers, supply pods, solar flares, dust storms and blood moons keep every run different.

Play the Campaign on five difficulty levels, build freely in Sandbox, or take on the Daily Challenge, where everyone gets the same planet and the same twist each day. Chase high scores, unlock 21 achievements, and fill in the alien codex.

Everything you see and hear is generated in your browser: no image or sound files, just code. It runs on your phone with no install, saves automatically, and never tracks you.

### Feature bullets
- Factory automation and tower defense in one mobile-first game
- One-swipe belt building, blueprints and a 35-node research tree
- 8 alien species, a Leviathan boss every fifth night, and pollution that provokes raids
- 24 campaign missions and 4 commander powers
- 6 random events, including meteor showers that leave rich ore
- Campaign, Sandbox and Daily Challenge modes, 5 difficulty levels
- High scores, 21 achievements, an alien codex and screenshot sharing
- Adaptive music and sound, all synthesized live
- English and Korean, autosave, no ads and no tracking

### Controls
- **Touch:** drag to move the map, pinch to zoom, tap a building to inspect it, pick a building and drag to place a whole line.
- **Desktop:** drag to move, mouse wheel to zoom, `R` rotate, `X` demolish, `B` blueprint, `Space` pause, `Esc` or right-click to cancel.

## Publishing the HTML5 build on itch.io
1. Get a build: run `npm install && npm run build`, or download the `dist` artifact from the latest green CI run on GitHub Actions.
2. Zip the **contents** of `dist/` so that `index.html` sits at the root of the zip.
3. On itch.io, create a new project and set **Kind of project** to **HTML**.
4. Upload the zip and tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
5. Embed options: viewport 1280 × 720 for desktop, tick **Mobile friendly** (orientation: Default), tick **Fullscreen button**, leave scrollbars off.
6. Paste the description, tags and screenshots from this file, then publish.

The build uses relative paths, so it needs no changes to run on itch.io. Saves are stored per website, so a colony started on GitHub Pages does not appear on itch.io. Players can move it with **Menu → Export JSON** and **Import JSON**.
